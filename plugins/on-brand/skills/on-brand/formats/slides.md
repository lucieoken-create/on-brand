# Slides

Starter rules. Fill in the brand-specific lines when writing a system.

## Size

1920 × 1080 (16:9). Keep text at least 96px from the left and right edges and 64px from the top and bottom.

## Type sizes (for screen sharing and projectors)

- Slide title: {{headline font}} at 64 to 80px. Titles can run to two lines; keep them under about 30 characters per line.
- Body: {{body font}} at 28px minimum. Small text and captions at 18px minimum.

## Rules

- One idea per slide, and the title says it. Body under 60 words. If a slide needs a third paragraph, it's two slides.
- Every slide has a real title, including image slides.
- Emphasize one line per slide at most, with the accent color or a highlight, not bold everywhere.
- {{Their copy rules: case, punctuation, banned words.}}
- Cover, section dividers and closing slide use {{background color}}; content slides use {{background color}}.
- Credit any image or quote you didn't make in a small caption.

## Making the file

Build as HTML (one section per slide at 1920 × 1080) or in their slide tool of choice following these rules. Check every slide at full size before sharing: no text touching the edges, nothing overflowing, every slide titled.
