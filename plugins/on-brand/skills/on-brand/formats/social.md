# Social posts, banners and event covers

Starter rules. When writing a brand's system, fill in the brand-specific lines and delete anything that conflicts with their decisions.

## Keep the specs current

Platforms change sizes and safe zones without much notice, and spec guides online often disagree. So this file records when its specs were last checked.

**Specs last checked:** 2026-11-11 (on-brand starter set). **Checked against:** platform help centers where available, cross-checked with recent spec guides.

Before making a piece:

1. If the date above is more than about three months old, or the platform or placement isn't in the table, and you can search the web, look up the current spec on the platform's own help pages first: Instagram Help Center or Meta Business Help, LinkedIn Help, TikTok Support, Pinterest Business Help, YouTube Help, Luma Help, Eventbrite Help. Use third-party spec guides only to fill gaps, and prefer ones that say when they last checked.
2. If sources disagree, use the platform's own page. If it isn't clear, pick the size that crops least badly (keep words in the center) and say what you weren't sure about.
3. Update the table and the date above, and tell the person in one line what changed.
4. If you can't search the web, use the table as it is and say when it was last checked.

## Sizes

| Platform and placement | Size (px) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram feed, portrait | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | The default. The app also accepts 3:4 (1080 × 1440), but scheduling tools usually stop at 4:5, so 4:5 works everywhere. |
| Instagram feed, square | 1080 × 1080 | For mixing with older square posts. |
| Instagram carousel | Same as feed | Every slide uses the first slide's shape. Keep one shape for the whole set. |
| Instagram story or reel cover | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Keep words out of the top 250px and bottom 340px, where the app's buttons sit. |
| LinkedIn post image | 1080 × 1350 or 1200 × 627 | Portrait takes more room in the feed. 1200 × 627 suits link previews and page posts. |
| LinkedIn document (carousel) post | A PDF, 1080 × 1350 per page by convention | LinkedIn doesn't publish a page size. |
| LinkedIn personal banner | 1584 × 396 | The profile photo covers the lower left on desktop; keep words to the right. |
| LinkedIn company page cover | Check before making | Sources currently disagree (1512 × 256, 4200 × 700 and 1128 × 191 all circulate). Check LinkedIn Help's page image specifications. |
| Luma event cover | Square, 1:1 (1080 × 1080 or larger) | Luma recommends square for event pages, explore feeds and shares. |
| Eventbrite event image | 2160 × 1080 (2:1) | Also cropped to a square thumbnail, so keep the subject centered. Check before making. |

Keep every word at least 60px from the edges on feed posts.

## Hierarchy

One thing is biggest. On an event or sale post that's usually the headline or the offer; the date comes second; the brand name or handle is smallest.

- Headline: {{headline font}} at 88 to 120px on a 1080-wide post. Shorter headlines go bigger.
- Supporting line (date, offer, detail): {{body font}} at 36 to 44px.
- Small text (handle, location): 26 to 30px. Nothing smaller than 24px; it won't read on a phone.
- Banners are wide and short: one line plus a handle or web address, sized to read on a phone.

## Every post ships with a caption

The caption carries what didn't fit: dates and times, price, address, and where the link is ("link in bio" or the story link sticker). Write it to the copy rules in PRODUCT.md.

## A set of posts

For a campaign (an event, a sale), make the set look related: same layout family, same accent use, one changing element (date, product, quote). Label each one (Feed post A, Story B) when showing them.
