# Social posts

Starter rules. When writing a brand's system, fill in the brand-specific lines and delete anything that conflicts with their decisions.

## Sizes

| Format | Size (px) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed post, portrait | 1080 × 1350 | The default. Takes the most room in the feed. |
| Feed post, square | 1080 × 1080 | For carousels that mix with older square posts. |
| Story or reel cover | 1080 × 1920 | Keep text out of the top 250px and bottom 340px, where the app's buttons sit. |
| LinkedIn post | 1200 × 1500 or 1080 × 1350 | Portrait does better than landscape in the feed. |

Keep every word at least 60px from the edges on feed posts.

## Hierarchy

One thing is biggest. On an event or sale post that's usually the headline or the offer; the date comes second; the brand name or handle is smallest.

- Headline: {{headline font}} at 88 to 120px on a 1080-wide post. Shorter headlines go bigger.
- Supporting line (date, offer, detail): {{body font}} at 36 to 44px.
- Small text (handle, location): 26 to 30px. Nothing smaller than 24px; it won't read on a phone.

## Words on the image

- Headline: {{their limit, default six words or fewer}}.
- No more than {{their limit, default four or five}} short phrases on the image in total.
- On the image: {{e.g. the headline, the date, the offer}}. In the caption: {{e.g. the details, address, link location, hashtags}}.
- Price on the image is a call for each post: it can help, but leaving it off keeps the graphic usable if the price changes. When it's off the image, the caption carries it.

## Color

Background in {{neutral or primary}}. The accent ({{tertiary}}) on one thing only, usually the date or the offer. Text color from the passing pairs in DESIGN.md.

## Photos

{{Their photo rules: own photos only, no stock, never text over faces, etc.}} If text sits on a photo, put it on a solid band of a palette color, not straight over the image.

## Every post ships with a caption

The caption carries what didn't fit: dates and times, price, address, and where the link is ("link in bio" or the story link sticker). Write it to the copy rules in PRODUCT.md.

## A set of posts

For a campaign (an event, a sale), make the set look related: same layout family, same accent use, one changing element (date, product, quote). Label each one (Feed post A, Story B) when showing them.
