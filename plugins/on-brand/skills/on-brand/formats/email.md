# Email headers and newsletters

Starter rules. Fill in the brand-specific lines when writing a system.

## Sizes

- Header image: 600px wide on screen. Export at 1200px wide so it stays sharp on phones, and keep it under about 250px tall at 600 wide.
- Keep any words on the header large: at least 32px at 600 wide.

## Rules

- Put the important words in live text, not in the image. Many inboxes block images until the reader allows them, and images can't be read by screen readers. Give every image short alt text.
- One header style for the series, with the issue or event changing.
- Logo or business name in the header, small.
- Buttons in {{accent color}} with {{button text color}} text, written to the call-to-action style in PRODUCT.md.
- Some inboxes switch to dark mode and flip colors. Avoid a header that's mostly white with thin dark text; give it its own background color.
