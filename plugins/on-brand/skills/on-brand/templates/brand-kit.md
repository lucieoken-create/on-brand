## Canva

Canva keeps your brand in a **Brand Kit** (open **Brand** in the left sidebar on the home page).

1. Add the colors above, using the hex codes.
2. Add the two fonts: search for each in Canva's font list. Set them as your heading and body fonts.
3. Upload the logo files.

On the free plan, the Brand Kit only holds a few colors. Fonts and logos need Canva Pro, Teams or Enterprise. On free, keep this sheet handy and pick the colors by hex code instead.

To finish a piece Claude made in Canva, upload the PDF or PowerPoint file: Canva opens it as an editable design.

## Adobe

- **Illustrator, Photoshop or InDesign:** load `kit/brand-colors.ase` from the Swatches panel menu (Open Swatch Library > Other Library in Illustrator; Import Swatches in Photoshop; Load Swatches in InDesign). From there, you can add the colors to a Creative Cloud Library.
- **Adobe Express:** Brand kits are a Premium feature (included with some Creative Cloud plans). Add the colors, fonts and logos above to a new brand, or turn a Creative Cloud Library into a brand from its menu ("Use as Brand"). On the free plan, use this sheet and pick colors by hex code.
- **Fonts:** {{if Google Fonts: install them from `assets/fonts/` or fonts.google.com. Many Google Fonts are also in Adobe Fonts.}}

To finish a piece in Adobe Express, upload the PDF or PowerPoint file. Illustrator and InDesign open PDFs too.

## When the brand changes

Ask your brand skill to make the change. It updates DESIGN.md, this sheet and the swatch file together. Then update your Canva or Adobe brand kit to match.
